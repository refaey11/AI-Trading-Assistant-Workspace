## Pattern contracts
C002 Hammer: clear downtrend; small real body near candle top; lower shadow at least 2x real body; upper shadow very small/absent; bullish confirmation required.
C005 Shooting Star: after uptrend; small real body; upper shadow at least 2x real body; lower shadow very small/absent; bearish confirmation mandatory.
C101 Bullish Engulfing: after downtrend; bearish first candle; bullish second candle; second real body completely engulfs first real body; shadows need not be engulfed; confirmation required.
C102 Bearish Engulfing: mirror bearish construction after uptrend; confirmation required.
C108 Morning Star: downtrend; long bearish first candle; small star; strong bullish third candle closing well into first body; bullish confirmation required.
C109 Evening Star: uptrend; long bullish first candle; small star; strong bearish third candle closing well into first body; bearish confirmation required.
