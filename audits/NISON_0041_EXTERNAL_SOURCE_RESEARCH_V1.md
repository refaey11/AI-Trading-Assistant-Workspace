# Nison 0041 — External Source Research V1

Date: 2026-08-17
Purpose: determine whether the four previously qualitative pattern families can be made more operational without tuning on project data.

## Findings

1. Hammer / Hanging Man source material attributed to Steve Nison repeatedly states: small real body near the top of the range, very long lower shadow, little/no upper shadow; for a classic hammer the lower shadow should be at least twice the real-body height. Nison also explicitly notes that the 2x/3x form is an ideal and that a 1.5x example can still be interpreted differently by practitioners. Therefore 2x is source-supported as a classic/ideal criterion, but should not be silently converted into an absolute universal Nison threshold.

2. Shooting Star source material attributed to Nison states: small real body at the lower end of the range, long upper shadow, very small lower shadow; the upper shadow is at least twice the real-body height in reproduced criteria. Nison also discusses gap context as ideal/strengthening rather than universally mandatory in examples.

3. Morning Star: Nison describes a tall/long bearish first real body, followed by a small real body that gaps lower, followed by a strong white real body moving well within the first black body. The second gap is described as ideal/rare and its absence does not invalidate the formation. A public reproduction of Nison's text confirms this framing.

4. Evening Star is the bearish counterpart: long white first real body, star, then black real body moving sharply into the first white body. The source framing again uses qualitative terms such as long, small, strong and sharply/well into.

5. Nison's own discussion explicitly warns that these interpretations are subjective/general guidelines and that there are no concrete rules for every qualitative boundary. This is the critical governance point for the project.

## Operational decision

- Promote only criteria that are explicitly source-quantified (e.g. classic hammer/shooting-star shadow >= 2x real body) as source-supported constraints.
- Do NOT invent numeric cutoffs for 'small', 'long', 'strong', 'very small', or 'well into'.
- Do NOT use 2016-2024 outcomes to choose those cutoffs; that would be tuning/calibration.
- Keep unsupported qualitative predicates as NOT_EVALUABLE until an authoritative project source supplies an operational definition.
- 2025 remains OOS and excluded from tuning, calibration, optimization, and operator selection.

## Verdict

External research strengthens the source contract and permits additional source-supported constraints, but it does NOT justify converting all four patterns into fully deterministic binary predicates. 0041 therefore remains partially evaluable unless an authoritative source in the project's own knowledge base supplies the missing qualitative operators.
