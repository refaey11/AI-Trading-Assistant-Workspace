## 2. Chapter-by-chapter functional map

### Chapter 1 — Introduction to Technical Analysis
Core role: technical analysis philosophy, price/supply-demand orientation, technical vs fundamental analysis, timing and leverage.
Decision-Brain relevance: establishes that technical analysis is a market-evidence framework; does not itself define 0006/0007.

### Chapter 2 — Dow Theory
Core role: six basic tenets, trend hierarchy, averages, volume confirmation, reversal/closing-price concepts.
Decision-Brain relevance: trend hierarchy and confirmation context; useful upstream context, not a numeric 0006/0007 operator.

### Chapter 3 — Chart Construction
Core role: line/bar/point-and-figure/candlestick charts, OHLC construction, multiple timeframes, arithmetic vs logarithmic scales, volume/open interest construction.
Decision-Brain relevance: critical data/representation layer; confirms OHLC and timeframe compression concepts.

### Chapter 4 — Basic Concepts of Trend
Core role: trend definition/classification, support/resistance, psychology, trendline construction and breakout filters.
Key source text in supplied archive: up trendline connects successive reaction lows; down trendline connects successive reaction highs; tentative line requires 2 points; confirmed trendline requires a 3rd successful touch and reaction without breaking.
Important filters present in the supplied Chapter 4 material: price filters including a 3% rule for major trends / 1% for short-term, and a 2-day rule requiring two consecutive closes beyond the trendline.
Governance warning: the archive does NOT state that these general filters are automatically the numeric implementation for Murphy 0006/0007. They must not be silently bound to those rules without rule-specific compatibility evidence.

### Chapter 5 — Major Reversal Patterns
Core role: reversal structure, head-and-shoulders, inverse H&S, double/triple tops and bottoms, V/spike and rounding patterns, volume/confirmation context.
Decision-Brain relevance: pattern structure and confirmation; separate from 0006/0007 trendline operator.

### Chapter 6 — Continuation Patterns
Core role: triangles, flags/pennants, wedges, rectangles, cup-and-handle, continuation gaps, volume confirmation.
Decision-Brain relevance: continuation-pattern module; not a substitute for trendline confirmation semantics.

### Chapter 7 — Volume and Open Interest
Core role: volume, OBV, open interest, COT, options, climaxes, volume confirmation.
Decision-Brain relevance: confirmation/evidence layer. Important distinction: volume confirms price action; it does not automatically define 0006/0007.

### Chapter 8 — Long-Term Charts
Core role: long-term daily/weekly/monthly perspective, continuation charts, contract continuity, long-term patterns, top-down analysis, macro vs execution timeframes.
Key principle: top-down workflow from monthly to weekly to daily to intraday; long-term charts provide context, while shorter charts support timing/execution.
Decision-Brain relevance: supports multi-timeframe architecture and separation of context from execution.

### Chapter 9 — Moving Averages
Core role: SMA/other moving averages, crossovers, envelopes/Bollinger Bands, weekly rule/Donchian channels, time-cycle/Fibonacci relationships.
Decision-Brain relevance: secondary/confirmatory technical evidence; no authority to alter Murphy 0006/0007 semantics.

### Chapter 10 — Oscillators and Contrary Opinion
