# Hybrid Evidence — Murphy Canonical Integration Pilot V1

## Status
PILOT / NOT PRODUCTION

## Objective
Test whether engineering evidence can operationalize qualitative Murphy wording while preserving the existing canonical primitives and rule meaning.

## Three pilot concepts
1. Horizontalness / level quality
2. Convergence / parallelism
3. Breakout significance

## Integration rule
The pilot does not replace canonical primitives. It consumes their outputs and adds separately named engineering evidence.

### Horizontalness
Canonical evidence must establish the existence/order of the relevant structural boundaries. Engineering evidence may describe relative flatness/level quality; it must not redefine what the source means by the structure.

### Convergence / parallelism
Canonical evidence must establish the required boundaries and their ordering. Engineering evidence may quantify relative convergence/parallelism, but must not invent a new pattern definition.

### Breakout significance
Canonical evidence must establish the source-defined breakout event. Engineering evidence may describe relative penetration/magnitude and supporting context. It cannot turn a non-breakout into a breakout.

## Parameter governance
No engineering parameter may be selected using 2019–2024 outcome performance or 2025. Parameters must be declared/versioned before evaluation. Calibration may use 2016–2018 only.

## Required comparison
For each pilot rule record:
- baseline canonical status
- hybrid status
- engineering evidence fields
- provenance/version
- availability timestamp/bar
- prefix replay result
- future-suffix mutation result

## Success criterion
The hybrid is promoted only if it operationalizes a previously qualitative condition without changing canonical semantics, without lookahead, and without outcome-driven parameter selection.

## Failure criterion
Reject the hybrid for that rule if it creates a new semantic decision, needs evaluation-period tuning, or permits soft evidence to override a canonical failure.

## Decision output
The pilot produces evidence classifications only. It does not produce BUY/SELL.
