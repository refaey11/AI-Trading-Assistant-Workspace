- 2025 timestamp
- `mode=oos_evaluation`
- BUY/SELL/NO_TRADE decision
- entry/exit prices
- raw outcome R and applied cost R
- source rule IDs within the frozen allowlist

- `risk_pass=true` for executable decisions
