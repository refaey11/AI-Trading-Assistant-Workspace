# Trading in the Zone — 7 Rules Deferred Register V1

**Administrative decision:** These seven rules are formally deferred and must not block the AI Trading Assistant / Decision Brain project. Reopen only when the specified authoritative dependency becomes available.

## Canonical role
Trading in the Zone is a **psychology/process gate only**. It must never generate Buy/Sell direction or independently reverse market direction.

- **Reopen only if:** A source-locked, authoritative pre-entry risk evidence producer is integrated.
- **Guardrail:** TIZ remains psychology/process only; no directional output.
